Ask the right LUNA questions

You are an Operations Manager preparing for a cross-functional decision meeting. Use LUNA to identify the questions the team should discuss before committing.

For each of two decisions, sort 18 questions into the four LUNA lenses. Allocate 16 and leave the two that don’t fit in the bank. Consider the decision across functions, including Sales, Finance, and your supply network.

What you’ll do

  3. 3Leave the two distractions in the bank. Check answers to see feedback beside your choices, or compare all 18 answers together.

Ask before you commit

LUNA isn't a checklist to fill in after a decision is made—it's a set of questions to ask before you commit. Some things worth debating (like a slogan or a color) aren't LUNA questions at all, and knowing the difference keeps the framework sharp instead of diluted.
